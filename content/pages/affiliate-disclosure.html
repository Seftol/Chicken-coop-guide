Affiliate disclosure

[[site_name]] may later earn a commission if a reader buys through certain links. The programs under consideration are Omlet through Awin, Amazon Associates, Chewy, and Tractor Supply. We have not joined them. There are no affiliate links on the site, and no tracking IDs are in use.

When a page does contain an affiliate link, three things will be true:

  • The page will show a short notice at the top. That notice is added by the build, not typed in by hand, for every page whose metadata says it contains affiliate links.
  • The link will use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and open in a new tab. Writers mark those links in the page source; the build adds the attributes.
  • A commission, if one is paid, does not change the price the retailer charges. It also does not change a figure quoted from an extension service or a welfare organisation.

Ordinary links to sources, to government pages, and to a product page that is not an affiliate link are not sponsored. A link to a product is not a test of that product, and it is not a claim that we keep chickens.

Editorial pages can exist with no affiliate links at all. The space guide is one of those. Whether a later page recommends something for sale is a separate decision from whether the sources on that page support the claim.
