[[site_name]]

This is a small reference for people planning a backyard flock in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, or Australia. The first question it answers is how much coop and run the birds need. A second page is a checklist for hens that have stopped laying. Flock planning is not written yet.

The figures come from university extension services and from poultry welfare organisations. They do not agree with each other. Where they do not, the page says so and links the source. Measurements are given in square feet and square metres.

This site does not keep chickens. It does not claim to have built coops, lived with a flock, or tested products. A number on a retail listing is checked against the published allowances, not against a test we did not run.

How much space do chickens need?

The calculator and the reference chart cover coop floor, run area, nest boxes, and roost length for 1–40 birds, with a chart for 2–20 standard hens.

Why did my chickens stop laying?

A checklist of the causes extension pages name, ordered by how many of those pages name each one, including short days, molt, and supplemental winter light.

Nothing here is a local law. Cities and landlords set their own rules, and those rules can be stricter than any welfare leaflet. The space page includes one Canadian example so that difference is visible.
