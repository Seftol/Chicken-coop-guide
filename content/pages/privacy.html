Privacy policy

[[site_name]] is a static website. It has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter, and no contact form. We do not run analytics, advertising scripts, or a cookie banner of our own, because the site does not set cookies.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not receive a copy of the host’s logs. Like other web hosts, GitHub receives ordinary connection data when your browser loads a page, such as an IP address and the address of the page. That processing is GitHub’s. Their statement is the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The coop calculator runs in your browser. The flock size you type is not sent to us. It is not stored.

Links to extension services, welfare organisations, and other sites leave this site. Those sites have their own policies.

There are no affiliate links today. If a later page adds one, the retailer, not this site, may set a cookie or record that the click came from here. Those pages will show a short notice at the top, and the link will be marked. The affiliate disclosure explains that arrangement. This policy will be updated when that happens, rather than describing cookies the site does not use yet as if they were already here.
