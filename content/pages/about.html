About

[[site_name]] is an independent reference. It reads public sources, names them, and shows the arithmetic. It is not a farm, a store, a veterinary service, or a brand.

We do not keep chickens. We do not claim hands-on testing of coops, feeds, or equipment. If a page discusses a product listing, the discussion is the published floor area divided by the claimed flock size, compared with the sources on that page. That is not a product test.

Pages are written for readers in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Imperial and metric units are both shown. Spelling follows the unit: feet and inches for imperial, metres and centimetres for metric.

Sources are extension services, welfare charities, and, when a page is about a local rule, the government that published the rule. A blog that does not cite those kinds of sources is not used as evidence. Where two good sources disagree, both numbers stay on the page. They are not averaged into a third number nobody published.

The site may later carry affiliate links, including through Omlet via Awin, Amazon Associates, Chewy, and Tractor Supply. None of those programs have been joined, and no page uses an affiliate link today. Commercial relationships will be marked. They do not change a quoted source. Details are on the affiliate disclosure.

There is no contact form and no newsletter. The pages list their sources. Errors in a citation or a calculation can be reported on the source repository, which is also linked in the footer.
